# Round 2, path D: experience anchors

## The strongest objection

C, B and Fable make one point from three sides (round1_C.md:51, round1_B.md:60, round1_fable.md:49): answers given without a bottle carry almost no usable information. Each chip spreads over three or four families, visitors will tick several, and with no liked family `recommend()` ranks by avoidance alone. Fable adds that an anosmia question over-reports (round1_fable.md:20).

## Answer

Most of it holds; I re-ran it through tools/lib/site.js. With my own pseudo-entry, told "too sweet" and told "heavy" plus "smoky" both return Hermès H24, Afternoon Swim and Glacier Bold; an empty profile returns the first two plus Qaed Al Fursan. My brief already withheld picks for zero bottles (round1_D.md:7), but the answers also do little else.

The anosmia evidence changed my view. Bremner et al. (2003, Chemical Senses; abstract read via OpenAlex; cited at round1_fable.md:20) retested 9 of 55 putative androstenone non-detectors with forced choice: they detected it above chance despite low confidence, and the estimated true rate was 1.8 to 5.96 percent. The 30 to 37 percent at scent_and_chemistry_2022.txt:1992 comes from the same kind of single test. I withdraw the anosmia block; the question can stay only as a line pointing to a musk sample. I also drop the avoided-smells list, and the mapping of "made me feel sick" to "heavy", which measures sensitivity rather than a family (round1_A.md:53).

What survives is narrower. First, a complaint on each negative verdict. Re-run: Sauvage EDP stopped with Khamrah kept at +2 gives woody amber "mixed" (−0.67); adding "sharp / chemical" makes it "possible" (−0.94), as Fable found (round1_fable.md:14). The picks do not change, so the chip changes what the page says, not what it recommends. Second, the chip value fix: today a chip on a −2 verdict weakens its own families (round1_D.md:23), which affects every path that attaches chips, A and Fable included. Third, one bottle-free complaint question for zero-bottle visitors, used only to choose which diagnostic samples the starter set shows.

A's grid holds: re-run, it separates 88 of 90 ordered pairs of deciding families when strength means drydown 0.5 or heart 0.7 (round1_A.md:20); with drydown alone it is 77, because white florals reach strength only in hearts. Landmark verdicts do the separating; chips say which family in a bottle failed. D is therefore not the main structure. Its surviving parts belong inside A, as Fable proposed (round1_fable.md:5).

## Borda vote

1. **A.** Verdicts on worn landmark bottles are the most reliable signal, need no engine change, and its grid separates 88 of 90 family pairs on my re-run.
2. **D.** Its complaint on each negative verdict and the chip value fix are needed by any path built on verdicts, but its bottle-free inputs proved nearly empty.
3. **B.** It keeps stated answers out of exclusion and states clear falsifiers, but it asks this audience to judge unfamiliar materials by words, and unfamiliar odours were disliked 83 percent of the time (engen_odor_sensation_memory_1991.txt:870-871).
4. **C.** Lifestyle answers have no shown link to which materials a visitor rejects, and a type prior can only re-rank, never remove a disliked family.
